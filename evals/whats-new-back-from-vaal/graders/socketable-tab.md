Runes, soul cores, idols and ancient augments are kept in the Runes stash tab, not the currency tab; Perfect runes have no stash slot at all. The answer doesn't have to say where any of them are kept.

PASS if the answer names no rune, soul core, idol or augment, or names one without saying where it's kept, or says where one is kept and gets it right (the Runes tab, or for a Perfect rune the Currency Exchange).

FAIL if it says a rune, soul core, idol or augment is kept in the currency tab or any stash tab other than the Runes tab.
